  profile.jpg          your photo

  cert_python.jpg      Python certificate
  cert_cyber.jpg       Cyber Security certificate
  cert_hardware.jpg    Hardware & Networking diploma certificate
  Nipun_CV.pdf         your CV

Also edit the CONFIG block at the top of script.js (Web3Forms key, WhatsApp number, GitHub/LinkedIn/email),
and replace YOUR-USERNAME in index.html with your GitHub username.
